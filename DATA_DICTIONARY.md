# Data Dictionary (`What Fraction of the World's Watersheds are Ungauged?`)

This document describes the schemas and column definitions for the non-proprietary hydrological skill, topological gauge coverage, HydroATLAS out-of-distribution (OOD) representativeness, and TreeSHAP attribution datasets included in [`science_results/`](science_results/) and generated by the pipeline in [`cv_1995_2025/`](cv_1995_2025/) and [`science_tiers_and_figures/`](science_tiers_and_figures/).

> **Proprietary Data Exclusion Notice:**
> Commercial Fathom v3 flood hazard grids (`T in {5, 10, 20, 50, 100} yr`) and all Fathom-derived flood exposure datasets (`global_basin_exposure_by_return_period.csv`, `basin_exposure_and_blockers_v2.parquet`, `country_exposure_and_blockers_v2.csv`, `Table_S3_lead1.csv`, `anchored_3tiers_exposure_summary_lead1.csv`, `multi_mechanism_tiered_skill_and_exposure_lead1.csv`, etc.) are subject to Fathom's commercial data license and are **strictly excluded** from this repository and from external archives.

## 3. Anchored Gauge-Accessibility Tier Feature Parquets (`coverage_fractions/tier*.parquet`)

Each parquet file is indexed by HydroBASINS Level 12 watershed ID (`hybas_<id>`, `964,084` rows) and contains `9` purely topological and hydroclimatic columns (zero Fathom data):
- `total_upstream_coverage` ($\in [0, 1]$): Area-weighted fraction of upstream drainage area monitored by at least one independent upstream gauge in the tier.
- `max_upstream_coverage` ($\in [0, 1]$): Maximum single-gauge upstream nested drainage area ratio ($A_{\text{gauge}} / A_{\text{basin}}$) in the tier.
- `max_downstream_coverage` ($\in [0, 1]$): Maximum single-gauge downstream nested drainage area ratio ($A_{\text{basin}} / A_{\text{gauge}}$) in the tier.
- `upstream_gauge_count` (`int`): Count of maximal independent upstream gauges in the tier.
- `anomaly_score_if`, `anomaly_score_ocsvm`, `anomaly_score_lof` (`float`): Multivariate Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, and Local Outlier Factor anomaly scores computed in 88-dimensional standardized HydroATLAS space relative to the tier's gauge network.
- `U_net_tree` ($\in [0, 1]$): $1 - \max(\text{total\_upstream\_coverage}, \text{max\_downstream\_coverage})$.
- `U_net_prod` ($\in [0, 1]$): $(1 - \text{total\_upstream\_coverage})(1 - \text{max\_downstream\_coverage})$.

Key anchored tier files:
- `tier1a_active_post2016_ccby_8811.parquet` (`8,811` post-2016 active Caravan training gauges)
- `tier_caravan_estreams_34577.parquet` (`34,577` Open Community Compilation gauges: Caravan + EStreams + GRDC open academic)
- `tier_public_discharge_93827.parquet` (`93,827` Publicly Accessible Discharge gauges)
- `tier4_all_publicly_knowable_121819.parquet` (`121,819` All Publicly Knowable gauges)
- `tier5_universal_idealized_ceiling.parquet` (Idealized `100%` coverage + top-decile in-distribution reference)
- `tier0_zero_gauges.parquet` (Counterfactual zero-gauge baseline, $U_{\text{net}} = 1$ everywhere)

## 4. Hydrological Model Performance Metrics (`hydro_model_metrics/`)

Each CSV in `hydro_model_metrics/{gauged,random,separated,gauged_da,random_ft,random_da,random_ft_da,separated_ft,separated_da,separated_ft_da}/` is indexed by `gauge_id` (`8,811` post-2016 Caravan gauges) and evaluated over the 30-year window (`1995–2025`):
- **34 `lead_time = 1` metrics (`*_lead1.csv`)**:
  - `8` continuous hydrograph metrics: `NSE_lead1`, `KGE_lead1`, `log-NSE_lead1`, `log-KGE_lead1`, `Beta-NSE_lead1`, `Beta-KGE_lead1`, `Alpha-NSE_lead1`, `pearson-r_lead1`.
  - `26` flood return-period event metrics (`{T}-year-{W}-window-{precision|recall}_lead1` for $T \in \{1.01, 2.0, 5.0, 10.0, 20.0\}\text{ yr}$ and $W \in \{0.0, 1.0, 2.0\}\text{ days}$, avoiding duplicate $W=0$ columns).
- **34 peak-across-horizon metrics (`*.csv`)**: Corresponding 34 metrics evaluated on the maximum predicted discharge across forecast lead days 1–7.
