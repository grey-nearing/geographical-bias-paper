# Supplementary Data Files (Data S1–S2)

Machine-readable datasets accompanying *What Fraction of the World's Watersheds Are Ungauged?*

- **Data_S1_175_territories.csv**: Territory-level watershed counts, national flood-alerting status, fractional ungaugedness (land, population, and flood exposure under all 119,169 discoverable gauges), open-dataset gauges inside national borders, INFORM 2026 vulnerability and lack-of-coping-capacity scores, defended riverine flood exposure (Fathom v3 ≥10 cm), and forecast-coverage gains across gauge-access tiers under recall and CSI weighting (top 20 CAP-gap countries and group summaries in Table S4).
- **Data_S2_43_hydrological_metrics.csv**: Observed Caravan out-of-sample skill (medians and IQRs), major-basin-grouped 10-fold meta-model cross-validation R² (plus station, climate, and leave-one-continent-out diagnostics), and predicted global, alerting-country, and CAP-gap medians across all 43 hydrological metrics and local adaptation setups (core metrics summarized in Table S7).
